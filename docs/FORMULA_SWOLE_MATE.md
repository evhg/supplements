# Swole Mate: creatine stick, target formula v1

> **Superseded 2026-11-09 by `FORMULAS.md` (the day system).** Kept for the reasoning on the electrolyte base, creatine and calories.

_2026-11-09, from the user's creatine notes. Reference product: Neutonic Creatine Sticks
(7.5 g, micronised Creapure®, natural colour, flavour and sweetener, zero sugar)._

## Decisions

- **Flavoured only, no unflavoured version.** Unflavoured sells far less, and the flavour is the brand
  (cheeky names, PR look).
- **Creatine only, not 2-in-1 with electrolytes.** This keeps the range rule of one claim per stick.
  Electrolytes are Thirsty's job, and a buyer can take both. It also keeps the stick at ~7.5 g, the same
  format as Thirsty, so the OEM can use the same stick film and line.
- **Zero calorie,** like the whole range.

## Per stick

| Ingredient | Amount | Spec |
|---|---|---|
| **Creatine monohydrate** | **5.00 g** | micronised, ≥ 99.9% purity, certificate of analysis per batch. Creapure® (preferred) or Pürest™, see below |
| Acids | ~0.5–0.8 g | citric acid, malic acid |
| Sweetener | to taste | steviol glycosides (stevia; fermented Reb M for a cleaner taste), optional monk fruit |
| Flavour | to taste | natural flavouring only, carrier-free or non-caloric carrier |
| Colour | trace | beta-carotene (yellow/orange) or colouring foods: blackcurrant and carrot concentrate (red/pink). No artificial colours |
| Anti-caking | if needed | silicon dioxide |
| **Total** | **≤ 7.5 g** | everything except creatine ≤ 2.5 g |

**Example ingredient line:** Creatine Monohydrate (Creapure®), Acidity Regulators (Citric Acid, Malic Acid),
Natural Flavouring, Sweeteners (Steviol Glycosides), Anti-Caking Agent (Silicon Dioxide), Colour (Beta-Carotene).

**Calories:** creatine is normally declared as 0 kcal. The acids add ~1.5–2.5 kcal and the colour concentrates
a fraction of that, so the stick stays under the ~5 kcal budget. The OEM must confirm against the Thai rule
(same as Thirsty).

## Creapure vs Pürest

| | Creapure® (AlzChem, Germany) | Pürest™ (NNB Nutrition, China) |
|---|---|---|
| Track record | 20+ years; used in much of the research | launched Oct 2025 |
| Recognition | known to label-reading gym buyers (Neutonic leads with it) | none in Asia yet |
| Cost | premium | about half of other high-purity sources |
| Efficacy | same molecule; the differences are impurity levels, particle size and testing | same |

**Leaning Creapure.** The buyer for a ฿50-a-stick creatine is a label reader, and "Creapure®" on the stick does
the quality job for us. Decide once the OEM prices both. Using the Creapure® name and logo on pack may need
AlzChem's brand-partner approval; check before the label is designed.

## Things the OEM must confirm

1. **Thai FDA:** 5 g creatine per serving is allowed in our product category (no cap found, but the Wilder
   research flagged that creatine may need a different registration class), plus the required warnings.
2. **Mixing:** micronised creatine doesn't dissolve fully at 5 g in 300–500 ml. Agree what "mixes well" means
   (no gritty residue after 20 seconds of shaking) and test it.
3. **Taste:** creatine tastes faintly bitter. Flavour trials for the two flavours above.
4. **Price per stick** with Creapure vs Pürest, the MOQ, and whether the same stick format and line as Thirsty can be used.
5. **Creapure® on pack:** whether the OEM's Creapure supply comes with the right to use the trademark on our label.
