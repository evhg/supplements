# Supplements research handoff (from Claude chat, Nov 2026)

Context: research done while evaluating Wilder (Thai zero-sugar electrolyte sachet brand). Covers stick-pack product design, ingredients, Thai regulation, target markets, and cost benchmarks. Regulatory points are unverified and need a Thai regulatory consultant.

## 1. Stick-pack format limits

- Practical ceiling is about 10–12 g of powder per stick.
- Fits: electrolytes (~11 g), creatine (3–5 g), collagen (2.5–10 g; already the native format in Thailand), magnesium, most nootropic doses.
- Borderline: greens powders (8–15 g; fat stick, higher cost).
- Does not fit: protein (20–30 g per serving), mass gainers.

## 2. Product architecture

Flavoured electrolyte base plus one functional add-on per use occasion. One claim per stick; no 3-in-1 products (a third ingredient is a second claim and a second buyer).

| SKU | Add-on | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hydrate | none (the base) | |
| Power | creatine ~3 g | ~8–9 g total. Electrolyte matrix fixes creatine's poor mixability. Price above base (e.g. ฿60–65 vs ฿49) to avoid cannibalising |
| Energy | caffeine | Drop taurine: it pulls the product into Thai energy-drink classification and adds nothing users feel |
| Recover | magnesium glycinate + glycine + botanicals | Evening product; covers "sleep" legally. Soft flavour (chamomile-honey, not citrus). Low volume of liquid |

Cut: Focus (no venue logic, caffeine + L-theanine is just Energy, risky claims) and a separate Sleep SKU (same formulation as Recover).

Rules:
- Launch one SKU at a time; prove each to ~20% of revenue before the next.
- Once any SKU has caffeine, every SKU's label must state caffeine status.
- Lesson from Coke Zero / Zero Zero: a variant should remove one specific blocker for one specific occasion. Variants that just complete a matrix confuse the shelf.

## 3. Ingredient notes

### Creatine: Creapure vs Pürest

| | Creapure | Pürest |
|---|---|---|
| Maker | AlzChem, Germany | NNB Nutrition, China |
| Track record | 20+ years; used in much of the clinical research | Launched Oct 2025 |
| Purity claim | 99.9%, patented water-based process | Claims lowest dicyandiamide, dihydrotriazine, creatinine, cyanide |
| Testing | Long batch history | Certificate of analysis per batch |
| Particle | Standard and micronised | Micronised |
| Price | Premium benchmark | ~half of other high-purity sources |

- Efficacy is identical: it's one molecule. Differences are impurity levels (physiologically negligible at reputable grades), particle size (mixability), and verification.
- Spec to write into the purchase order: micronised, third-party certificate of analysis. Particle size matters more than the purity decimal.
- Creapure adds only ~฿1–2 cost per 3 g stick; worth it only if the target buyer reads labels. Pürest has no consumer recognition in Asia yet.

### Electrolyte formula comparison (per sachet)

| | Royal-D (market leader) | Wilder |
|---|---|---|
| Sachet | 25 g, in 250–400 ml | 10.9 g, in 400–600 ml |
| Base | Sugar (sucrose/dextrose first ingredient) | Zero sugar, fruit extracts |
| Sodium | ~70–75 mg | 1,000 mg (mainly sodium citrate) |
| Potassium | small | 600 mg |
| Magnesium | none | 100 mg |
| Extras | B and C vitamins (some versions), artificial colour | 40 mg L-theanine |
| Retail | ~฿5–10 | ~฿49.50 |

Formula is not a moat: a zero-sugar, high-sodium version from a big Thai player at ฿15 would erase it. The moat is brand and venue distribution.

## 4. Thai regulation (verify all of this)

- Melatonin is regulated as a drug, not a supplement.
- Caffeine plus taurine triggers energy-drink rules: caffeine caps, mandatory warnings.
- Cognitive ("focus") claims and hangover claims are risky; hangover claims are drug claims.
- Creatine as sports nutrition may fall under a different registration class than electrolytes.
- Halal certification needed for Indonesia and Malaysia.
- Collagen: crowded market, heavy 7-Eleven distribution, pulls a brand toward beauty.

## 5. Target markets beyond athletes

Ranked by size and fit for a premium stick sold through venues:

1. Tourists and travellers: hotels, minibars, airports, dive shops, ferries. Premium price accepted.
2. Nightlife recovery: bars, beach clubs, hotels. Position as "late nights", never "hangover".
3. Ramadan fasting hydration (halal): Indonesia and Malaysia. Biggest regional prize, annual spike, no premium brand owns it.
4. GLP-1 (weight-loss drug) users: reduced thirst; reach through clinics and telemedicine. Small, growing fast.
5. Heat-exposed workers: sell through employers, not retail. Royal-D's home turf.
6. Older adults: large but sold through caregivers and institutions. Later.

Skip: gamers, keto (small in Thailand), generic "wellness women" (collagen fight).
