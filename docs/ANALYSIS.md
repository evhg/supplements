## 1b. Two more Thai competitors (added 2026-11-09)

**BLUE ZONE by Slice Wellbeing (Phuket)** – Instagram @bluezone_hydration (~160 followers) and @slice_wellbeing.
- **Who makes it:** Slice Wellbeing Co., Ltd., a Phuket meal-plan and grab-and-go food company co-founded by chef
  David Laris. It sells from its own stores and from "Pick Up & Go" fridges in gyms, padel clubs, pilates studios and clinics.
- **Format:** a ready-to-drink bottle (as seen in their videos), not a powder stick. Bottle size and price aren't published.
- **Per serving** (Slice's own captions, Sep and Dec 2025): **sodium 190 mg (mineral sea salt), potassium 200 mg, magnesium 50 mg**,
  lemon + kaffir lime, **blue spirulina** for colour, "a touch of stevia". Zero sugar, "no fillers, no fake flavours".
- **Positioning:** "Clean electrolyte hydration with zero sugar, built for the Modern Performer. Find Your Limit. Then Move It."
- **Partners:** Destination Padel, Phuket United FC, Cherngtalay Run, Porto de Phuket, Bangkok Throwdown; also KĀHLĪ
  (a Phuket tennis/residence club) for Slice meal plans. Launched Sep 2025.
- **What it means for us:** a light, everyday dose (about a quarter of Wilder's sodium), sold through venues rather than retail.
  The spirulina blue and the Thai kaffir lime are good real-food ideas. Ours carries ~2–4× the electrolytes per serving,
  in a stick that travels and lasts.

**Simplify Electrolytes (simplify.co.th)**
- **Formula:** sodium chloride, **magnesium bisglycinate**, potassium chloride; zero sugar.
- **Two strengths:** 500 mg sodium (+250 mg potassium) or 1,000 mg sodium (+500 mg potassium).
- **Price:** a variety pack at ฿590; Thai FDA registered, HACCP. They sell a "sweat test" to choose a strength.
- **What it means for us:** proof that a "pick your dose" story sells in Thailand, which fits our day system.

## 2. Segment map

Two axes separate the brands: **sport ↔ everyday life** and **serious/clinical ↔ playful**.

```
                  SERIOUS / CLINICAL
                         │
          tenplus ●      │      ● HYDRTE
     (hypotonic, pro     │  (vitamin "complex",
        athletes)        │   wellness tourists)
                         │
          WILDER ●       │
   (heat, Hyrox, gym)    │
SPORT ───────────────────┼─────────────────── EVERYDAY LIFE
                         │
                         │     ◎ OPEN SPACE
                         │   playful, real-food,
                         │   everyday occasions
                         │   (afternoon slump, late
                         │    nights, evenings, travel)
                         │
                  PLAYFUL / FUN
     Royal-D: everyday but cheap and medicinal; not on this premium map
```

**Nobody premium is playful, and nobody premium is selling everyday occasions.** Every premium brand
sells "performance" with a sport-science voice. The open space is a fun, real-food brand for everyday
occasions, which suits cheeky names and Paleo Robbie's tone ("Nothing processed, nothing artificial").

## 3. What this means for us

1. **Don't fight Wilder on sport.** Wilder owns heat and Hyrox, with athletes, a community and 450 doors.
   Our advantages are price (−20%), fun and real-food credibility. Lead with everyday occasions, not race day.
2. **The formula alone won't win.** Royal-D shows a big player could make a ฿15 zero-sugar version. What protects
   us is the brand and Paleo Robbie's own customer base, which competitors can't buy.
3. **The PR promise sets the bar.** "Nothing artificial" means no sucralose/acesulfame and no artificial
   flavours or colours. Check the OEM base against this before anything else. If the samples use artificial
   sweeteners, the product can't carry the PR name.
4. **Pomelo is the one flavour Wilder doesn't have.** It's Thai, uncommon in electrolytes and easy to make memorable.
5. **Proof to borrow:** tenplus uses Informed Sport and athletes, HYDRTE uses hospitality menus.
   Ours should use PR's own customers: reviews in the app, and adding sticks to meal-plan subscriptions.

## 4. Paleo Robbie grocery: where the sticks go

**What PR sells today** (paleorobbie.com/grocery, 2026-11-08):

- Electrolytes sit under **Supplements → Other supplements → Hydration**, three levels deep. The only brand is
  **WILDER, all 5 flavours at ฿1,490 per 30 and a 12-variety pack at ฿695.** PR's site shows prices ex VAT,
  so ฿1,490 is about ฿1,594 with VAT, the same as Wilder's own ฿1,590.
- **There is no creatine at all.** Supplements cover protein powders, collagen, fish oil, superfood powders,
  ketones, mushrooms, CBD and organ capsules.
- Stick/sachet formats already sell: Plantogenic superfood drinks come in 7-sachet boxes at ฿390.
- No PR own-brand supplements yet. PR own-brand items so far are sauces, hot dogs and meat labels.

**Recommendation: create one category, "Hydration & Performance", and own it.**

- Move it out of "Other supplements" to be the **first sub-category under Supplements**, and cross-list
  it under **Coffee & beverages**, where people already shop for drinks.
- Order: PR sticks first, then Wilder. Because creatine is missing entirely, **PR Power (creatine) would be
  the only creatine on the site**, so the "Performance" half of the category is ours alone.
- Add a **sampler** under Deals → Samplers (e.g. 3-flavour 9-stick box) and a "+ add sticks" upsell in the
  meal-plan checkout.

**Things to sort out with Robbie:**

- **PR sells Wilder. Decided: Wilder stays.** Listed next to ours, Wilder is the reference point: the PR sticks
  look better on price (−20%), flavour (Pomelo) and the real-food promise. Expect some cannibalisation; keep the supplier relationship.
- **PR's own Wilder sales are the best demand data we have:** units per month, flavour mix and repeat rate.
  Ask Robbie for PR's sell-through. That's PR's own data, not Wilder's.
- **Price on PR:** Wilder ฿1,490 ex VAT → −20% = **฿1,190 ex VAT (≈ ฿1,273 incl.)**.
